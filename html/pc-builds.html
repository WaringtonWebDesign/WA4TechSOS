Common questions

Custom PC
questions.

Do I need to know what components I want?

No. Just tell us your budget and what you want the PC to do. We'll recommend suitable components and explain the choices to you.

How much does a custom PC cost?

There isn't one fixed price because every build is different. The cost depends on your budget, requirements and the components selected.

Can you build a gaming PC?

Yes. Gaming PCs are one of the main types of systems we can build, with the components selected around the games and performance you're looking for.

Can I choose the components?

Yes. If you already have particular components in mind, let us know. We'll check compatibility and help make sure the rest of the system works with them.
